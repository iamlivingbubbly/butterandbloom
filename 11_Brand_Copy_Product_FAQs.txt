SOAP  ($5.00 / 70 g)
-----------------------------------
Q. Is your soap vegan?
A. Yes — every bar is made from plant oils and plant butters. The only
process ingredient, sodium hydroxide, is fully consumed during saponification
and is never present in the finished bar.

Q. Is it really cold-process?
A. Yes. Each batch is mixed at a controlled temperature and left to cure at
least six weeks — that slow cure gives the bar a long-lasting body and plenty
of naturally occurring glycerin, which glycerin-boiling factory soaps lose.

Q. My skin is sensitive. Is this for me?
A. Our formula leans gently on olive oil and shea butter, both of which
sensitive skin tends to love. As with any new skincare, do a small patch test
first.

Q. How should I store it?
A. Between washes, keep the bar somewhere it can drain and dry. A dry bar
genuinely lasts months longer.

Q. How long does one bar last?
A. With basic care, a single 70 g bar typically lasts four to six weeks of
daily body use.

BATH BOMB  ($5.00 / 70 g)
-----------------------------------
Q. Is it vegan?
A. Yes — all of our bath bombs are made without any animal products, and the
oils we use are plant-based.

Q. Why does the tub get slippery?
A. The skin-kind sweet almond oil dispersing through the water is the culprit.
Take your time getting out, and when in doubt, rinse the tub.

Q. How long do they keep?
A. About six months when kept sealed away from moisture. A humid bathroom is
a fizz's worst enemy.

Q. Do they stain the tub or skin?
A. We use cosmetic-grade, skin-safe colorants in soft, translucent amounts —
they rinse away clean and leave nothing behind.

SHOWER STEAMER  ($8.00 / 340 g · 3 tablets)
-----------------------------------
Q. Do I need a bathtub?
A. No — that's the whole point. A shower steamer is aromatherapy for people
without a tub: place it on the floor and let the warm water do the rest.

Q. How long does one tablet last?
A. Each tablet fizzes on and off for the length of a normal shower — one
tablet per shower is usually plenty.

Q. Is it safe for kids?
A. Yes, with adult supervision, with the tablet placed out of a child's reach.
Essential-oil products should never be eaten, so keep them up and away from
little hands.

Q. What are they good for?
A. Congestion, stuffy winter sinuses, or simply turning a rushed morning
shower into a mindful two minutes. Eucalyptus Mint is the classic pick.

SUGAR SCRUB  ($5.00 / 450 g)
-----------------------------------
Q. Is it too rough for my skin?
A. Gritty enough to do the job — never harsh. If your skin feels tight
afterwards, ease up on pressure and amount; your skin will tell you what it
likes.

Q. Can I use it on my face?
A. We formulate it for the body. Facial skin is thinner, and a body scrub's
oil base can be too rich — stick to the neck down.

Q. How do I stop it from spoiling?
A. Keep water out of the jar above all else — scoop with completely dry hands.
Sealed and dry, it keeps for about a year.

Q. Which scent should I try first?
A. Vanilla Bean is our most-loved. Coffee is the morning pick, and Brown
Sugar Glow is the gentle sweet spot between the two.

LIP BALM  ($2.00 / 10 g)
-----------------------------------
Q. Is it really vegan?
A. Yes. Instead of beeswax or lanolin, we use candelilla wax — a plant wax —
with cocoa or mango butter in every tube.

Q. Does it contain tree nuts?
A. It contains sweet almond oil, a tree-nut derived oil. It's always clearly
labelled, so nut-sensitive households can decide with full information.

Q. Will it melt in my pocket?
A. Candelilla wax has a higher melt point than beeswax, so it holds its shape
in warm pockets far better than most natural balms.

Q. How much should I use?
A. One light pass is plenty — it's rich enough to last between applications
without constant reapplying.

WHIPPED BODY BUTTER  ($7.00 / 450 g)
-----------------------------------
Q. Why does it soften above 25°C?
A. Real shea butter and coconut oil are naturally temperature-sensitive —
that's a sign there's nothing fake in the jar. Give it a stir, or pop it in
the fridge for ten minutes.

Q. Is it greasy?
A. Not the way you expect. We set it with a touch of arrowroot powder, which
helps the butter melt in rather than sit on top of the skin.

Q. Is it gluten-free?
A. Yes — shea, coconut oil, sweet almond oil, arrowroot, and fragrance.
Nothing in the formula contains gluten.

Q. How much should I use?
A. A little goes a long way. Start with a fingertip-sized scoop, massage into
warm just-showered skin, and build from there.
