A. BUSINESS OVERVIEW (paste-ready summary)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Business name:  Butter & Bloom
Location:       Cornwall, Ontario (home-based, accessible production space)
Founder:        Owner/operator (33, woman, living with a disability)
Website:        Wix live store + custom "design your own" configurator
Industry code:  Handcrafted bath & body products (cosmetics/craft manufacturing)

Butter & Bloom is a handcrafted bath and body company making small-batch,
cold-process soap, bath bombs, shower steamers, sugar scrubs, lip balms and
body butter. Products are made in an accessible home studio, sold through
Ontario farmers' markets, the Cornwall region, and an online store with a
custom fragrance/configurator tool ("Design your own" - customer chooses
collection > product > scent > size, and the recipe is built around them).

Differentiators:
- Custom 4-step product configurator (collection, product, scent, size/name)
- Systematic fragrance architecture (top/middle/base notes per collection)
- Ingredient-transparent labels, Health Canada CNF-compliant
- Small batch = no preservative-heavy mass production; made-to-order freshness
