How to use this file:
- File ONE CNF per product within 10 days of first sale. Free, online:
  https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/consumer-product-safety/cosmetics/notification-cosmetics.html
- Enter ingredients by INCI name using the form's "Find INCI Name"
  button, and the concentration (exact % shown below is accepted, or use
  the range-code column).
- Check every ingredient against the Cosmetic Ingredient Hotlist.
- Tick "Fragrance Allergen" for flagged allergens (see end of file).
- Calculations below: based on the batch sizes in 03_Formulation_Sheets.
  Percentages are working estimates — adjust to your exact weighed batch.

=====================================================================
Flag on the form — and list on the product label — any of these present
above the threshold (leave-on >0.001% / rinse-off >0.01%) in your
fragrance or essential oils:
  Limonene · Linalool · Citral · Geraniol · Citronellol · Eugenol ·
  Cinnamal (cinnamon) · Cinnamyl Alcohol · Coumarin · Benzyl Alcohol
Common in your formulas: Limonene + Linalool (lavender, citrus, rose),
Citral (citrus), Eucalyptus oil (1,8-cineole, not a listed allergen but
follow IFRA usage rates).

====== NUT ALLERGEN REMINDER ======
Sweet Almond Oil (Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis) is a tree-nut allergen —
tell Health Canada via the notification and show it clearly on the label
(bath bombs, sugar scrub, lip balm, body butter). Use Jojoba where listed
for a nut-free version.

LABEL REQUIREMENTS (quick):
- Ingredients in INCI, in descending order of concentration
- Net quantity + your contact info (identity + principal place of
  business under CPLA) + primary brand and product name
- Batch code/date on every unit (you already do this)
