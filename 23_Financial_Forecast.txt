5. BREAK-EVEN
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Cash break-even (operating costs only, no draw):
    $14,000 / $4,230.60 per batch = 3.3 batches/yr = 0.28/mo = ~220 units/mo
  Comfortable break-even (incl. $12k owner draw):
    $26,000 / $4,230.60 = 6.1 batches/yr = 0.51/mo = ~410 units/mo
  Base plan runs 12 batches/yr (1.0/mo) - roughly double both break-evens.
  Margin is not the risk; sell-through volume is (see sensitivity).

6. SENSITIVITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  VOLUME (the binding constraint):
    Conserv. 9/18/27 batches   Rev $51.2k/102.4k/153.6k; Y1 still EBITDA +$24k
    Base    12/24/36 batches   Rev $68.3k/136.5k/204.8k
    Aggress 16/32/48 batches   Rev $91.0k/182.0k/273.0k (needs part-time help)

  PRICE:
    +$1.00 avg/unit  -> +$802 revenue/batch, gross margin 74.4%->77.5%
    Recommended tier -> +$936/batch  (best immediate lever; no volume cost)

  COSTS:
    Packaging -$0.30/unit -> +$241/batch  (biggest COGS lever, file notes)
    Ingredient +10%       -> -$45/batch   (negligible - grants pricing cushion)
    Shipping: 40% online bundles -> blended gross ~72%  (absorbed in Y1 mix)

  DOWN-SIDE TEST (worst credible):
    25% lower volume + 10% higher ingredient cost + 40% online-single mix:
    Y1 revenue ~$51k, gross ~$32k vs. total outlay ~$26k  -> still cash +
    materially. The model is robust because COGS is only 25.6% of revenue.
