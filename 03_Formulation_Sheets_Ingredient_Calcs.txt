HOT-PROCESS SOAP — MASTER FORMULA
===============================================================================

Uses same oil/lye/water ratios as Cold-Process.
Additional step: Cook in slow cooker on LOW until internal temp reaches 95°C+.

Cook Stages (approximate timing on LOW):
| Stage | Internal Temp | Time (approx) | Appearance |
|-------|---------------|---------------|------------|
| Trace | ~40°C         | 10–20 min     | Pudding-like |
| Gel   | ~70–80°C      | 30–45 min     | Translucent, waxy |
| Applesauce | ~85–90°C | 45–60 min    | Lumpy, wet |
| Vaseline | ~95–100°C   | 60–90 min    | Glossy, smooth |

Add fragrance at "Vaseline" stage, stir in, then spoon into mold.

BATH BOMBS — MASTER FORMULA (70 g x 4 Bombs = 280 g Batch)
===============================================================================

Dry Phase:
| Ingredient              | Weight (g) | Per Bomb (g) |
|-------------------------|------------|--------------|
| Baking Soda (NaHCO3)    | 120.0      | 30.0         |
| Citric Acid             | 60.0       | 15.0         |
| Cornstarch              | 15.0       | 3.75         |
| TOTAL DRY (excl. oil)   | 195.0      | 48.75        |

Wet Phase:
| Ingredient              | Amount |
|-------------------------|--------|
| Sweet Almond or Jojoba Oil | 7.5 mL |
| Fragrance/Essential Oil  | 2.5–3.75 mL |
| Polysorbate 80           | 3.75 mL |
| Witch Hazel (spray bottle)| As needed |
| Colorant (mica or liquid) | As desired |

Citric Acid to Baking Soda Ratio = 1:2 (by weight)
Finished weight per bomb with wet phase = ~70 g

Wet-to-Dry Squeeze Test:
Pinch mixture, squeeze — if it holds shape without crumbling, it's ready.
If crumbles → mist with witch hazel (1–2 sprays at a time)
If fizzing → too wet, add more dry mix

LIP BALM — MASTER FORMULA (10 x 10 mL Tubes) — VEGAN
===============================================================================

| Ingredient              | Amount    | Notes |
|-------------------------|-----------|-------|
| Candelilla Wax          | 15 g      | Vegan wax (replaces beeswax) |
| Cocoa or Mango Butter   | 15 g      | Mango = vegan alternative |
| Sweet Almond Oil        | 30 mL     | Carrier oil |
| Cosmetic Flavor/EO      | 5–10 drops | Suitable cosmetic flavor or selected EO |
| TOTAL                   | ≈60 g     | ~10 x 10 mL tubes (6 g each) |

Note: Candelilla wax is plant-based (from the candelilla shrub), making this
formulation fully vegan when mango butter is used.

Variations:
| Variation | Butter Swap | Flavor / EO Add-In |
|-----------|-------------|--------------------|
| Vanilla | Cocoa butter | Vanilla flavor |
| Mint    | Cocoa butter | Peppermint EO (2–3 drops) |
| Berry   | Mango butter | Berry flavor |
| Citrus  | Mango butter | Sweet orange flavor/EO |
| Rose    | Cocoa butter | Rose flavor, rose petal powder |

Pouring Notes:
- Melt wax first, then add butter, then oil
- Remove from heat before adding flavor/EO (EOs are volatile)
- Pour immediately into tubes — wax sets fast
- Fill to just above the rim (slight dome as it cools)

WHIPPED BODY BUTTER — MASTER FORMULA (4 x 4 oz Jars)
===============================================================================

| Ingredient              | Amount   | Notes |
|-------------------------|----------|-------|
| Shea Butter             | 200 g    | Base butter |
| Coconut Oil             | 100 g    | Softens texture |
| Sweet Almond Oil        | 60 mL    | Carrier oil |
| Fragrance Oil           | 15–20 mL | Or essential oils |
| Arrowroot Powder        | 15–30 g  | Sets consistency, reduces greasiness |
| TOTAL                   | ≈400 g   | ~4 x 4 oz (113 g) jars |

Method:
1. Melt shea and coconut together (low heat, double boiler)
2. Add sweet almond oil, stir
3. Cool to room temp → refrigerate until opaque and semi-solid
4. Whip with hand mixer (3–5 min) until fluffy
5. Add fragrance + arrowroot powder during whipping
6. Whip to soft-serve consistency
7. Spoon into jars, cap, label

Body Butter Variations:
| Variation | Swap/Add-In | EO Blend |
|-----------|-------------|----------|
| Lavender Dream | Same | Lavender 8 mL + Cedarwood 4 mL |
| Citrus Sunrise | Same | Sweet Orange 10 mL + Lemon 5 mL |
| Vanilla Bean | Add 1 tsp vanilla bean paste | Vanilla fragrance 15 mL |
| Fresh Garden | Same | Rosemary 5 mL + Peppermint 3 mL + Tea Tree 3 mL |
| Baby Soft | Replace coconut with jojoba oil | Chamomile (or unscented) |

===============================================================================
SAFETY NOTES
===============================================================================

LYE (Sodium Hydroxide):
- Always add lye TO water, never water to lye
- Work in well-ventilated area
- Wear goggles, gloves, long sleeves
- Lye solution heats to ~90°C — keep away from children
- Store in sealed, labeled container away from moisture
- Have white vinegar accessible (neutralizes lye burns)

CITRIC ACID:
- Avoid inhaling dust — work in ventilated area
- Can irritate eyes and skin
- Wear mask when mixing bath bomb dry ingredients

ESSENTIAL OILS:
- Never apply undiluted to skin
- Keep away from eyes
- Check for photo-sensitivity (citrus EOs)
- Store in cool, dark place
- Keep out of reach of children
