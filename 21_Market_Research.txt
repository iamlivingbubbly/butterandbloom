1. MARKET SIZE — GLOBAL
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Handmade soap market: USD 8.33B (2024) -> 12.49B (2034), CAGR ~5.2%.
    Drivers: awareness of harsh chemicals in mass-market soaps, e-commerce
    reach, and the premiumization trend. (Zion Market Research, Aug 2026)
  Soap market (all): USD 51.69B (2025) -> 82.61B (2035), CAGR 4.80%.
    Trends: herbal/organic bars, sustainable packaging, specialized skin
    needs. (Expert Market Research, Aug 2026)
  Vegan cosmetics market: USD 19.8B (2025) -> 36.41B (2034), CAGR 7.0%.
    Skincare = 39.6% of segment revenue in 2025 (the largest slice), and
    skincare is the category's fastest product type. (Straits Research, Aug 2026)
  Natural cosmetics market: USD 23.19B (2025) -> 40.63B (2034), CAGR 6.43%.
    North America holds 34.8% of the global natural cosmetics market.
    (Straits Research, 2026)
  Bath bomb market: established global segment; key brands Lush, Da Bomb,
    Pearl Bath Bombs; priced tiers mass/medium/premium. (Cognitive Market
    Research, Jul 2026)

2. MARKET SIZE — CANADA (our sell-in market)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Canada natural & organic personal care: USD 1.749B (2025) -> 3.033B (2034),
    CAGR 6.34%. Skin care is the leading product type; shower products are
    the FASTEST growing type in Canada — meaning bath/body fits the wave.
    (Deep Market Insights, Jun 2026)
  Canada clean beauty: USD 109.2M (2026) -> 163.2M (2036), CAGR 4.1%.
    Ingredient-led brands get a repeat-purchase path from familiar routines.
    (Future Market Insights, Aug 2026)
  Canada beauty retail: prestige beauty H1 2026 = $2.2B CAD, +6% yoy.
    Skincare +6%; premium body care gaining as consumers extend facial
    routines to body; ONLINE = >75% of the category's dollar growth; body
    oils +26%; masstige (mid-range) +22% — massively outperforming prestige.
    (Circana, Aug 2026)
  Canada bath soap segment: ~9.2% CAGR (fastest in North American bath
    soap), supported by Canada's ~40M population with high per-capita
    personal-care spend and strong preference for premium/organic.
    (Market Data Forecast, Jul 2026)

3. DEMAND TRENDS WE RIDE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Clean/vegan/cruelty-free is table stakes, not a differentiator alone.
     Purchase hinges on ingredient transparency + proof (Canada: clean-
     label demand rising; labelling guidance Nov 2025, allergen rules
     tightening Apr 2026 -> 81 allergens Aug 2026).
  2. Premiumization & "masstige": mid-priced quality beating prestige;
     premium soap expected +7.4% (NA). Our price ladder sits mid-premium.
  3. Artisanal/local: growth of artisanal, locally sourced handcrafted soaps
     (NA bath soap report); Etsy handmade soap sales +68% (2023 US).
     "Made in Ontario" and hometown Cornwall carry real premium weight.
  4. Gifting & self-care: bath/body is Canada's top gifting-within-craft
     category; holiday Q4 concentrates ~40-55% of artisan revenue.
  5. Online dominance in beauty growth + social discovery (why the site +
     short-form video plan).
  6. Body-oil/body-care ritual expansion (+26%) — room for body butter and
     scrub as "body-face" steps.

4. CUSTOMER RESEARCH (who buys)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Buyer profiles, ranked by revenue potential for B&B:
    P1 "Mindful Indulger" (30-55, F) — buys vegan/clean for self + gifting;
      premium okay, wants proof and story. Primary online + boutique buyer.
    P2 "Market Regular" (25-65, local) — recurring craft-market shopper;
      wants to support local, values face-to-face trust and refill/knowledge.
    P3 "Gift Giver" (35-65, F, seasonal) — Q4 spikes; bundles, 3-piece sets,
      lip balms as stocking stuffers.
    P4 "Wedding/Corporate" (brides, planners, HR) — custom favors/bulk;
      high ticket, project-based margin >70%.
    P5 "Tourist" (St-Lawrence/SDG corridor) — socca of local "made in
      Ontario" as a take-home, impulse $15-30.
  Observed purchase triggers (Etsy/artisan data): strong visual identity,
  ingredient names, small-batch/ethos proof, and giftability.

6. KEY COUNTRY-LEVEL FACTS FOR PITCH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - Canada market runs on trust + regulation clarity: natural and synthetic
    ingredients are treated as equally suitable (Health Canada, Mar 2025);
    shopper decisions increasingly driven by labelling + claims precision.
  - Bilingual FR/EN + INCI ingredient labelling is now the norm (Nov 2025
    guide), and allergen disclosure rules are getting stricter over 2026.
    Compliance = credential, not just burden, for a premium Canadian brand.
  - Canada's beauty is recession-resilient: +6% yoy through H1 2026 despite
    consumer caution (Circana) — evidence for our cash-flow story.
