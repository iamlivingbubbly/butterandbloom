DISABILITY-SPECIFIC + TAX & BENEFITS (often worth more than a grant)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
H) DISABILITY TAX CREDIT (DTC) -> gateway credit that unlocks:
   - RDSP: up to ~$70,000 in lifetime federal grants/bonds (free money into savings)
   - Canada Disability Benefit (CDB): up to ~$200/mo - Ontario pays it WITHOUT clawback
   - Canada Workers Benefit (CWB) eligibility is separate (income-based)
   - 2026 federal reforms are expanding DTC access + auto-filing

I) ODSP + SELF-EMPLOYMENT (if on ODSP):
   - First $1,000/month NET self-employment earnings fully exempt
   - Above $1,000: 75% clawback (keep 25% of each extra dollar)
   - Deduct real business expenses against revenue first (reduces chargeable income)
   - Essential tools of trade are exempt from the asset limit
   - Report income monthly (use CPA/ODSP forms); funded training (Opportunities Fund) is NOT income
   - Working ALWAYS leaves you better off than ODSP alone

J) FILE TAXES (even if income is low):
   - Claim CWB (refundable credit for self-employment income)
   - Claim eligible business expenses (ingredients, packaging, tools, market fees, car km,
     home workspace, insurance)
   - GST/HST credit; Ontario Trillium Benefit if renting
   - Business Number + register for GST/HST once revenue is expected > $30k/yr
