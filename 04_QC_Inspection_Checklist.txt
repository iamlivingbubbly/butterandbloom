Ingredient-Specific Checks:

Oils & Butters:
  [ ] No rancid/off smell
  [ ] Correct color (not darkened or cloudy)
  [ ] Consistency appropriate for type

Lye (NaOH):
  [ ] Pellet/flake form (not clumped or powdered)
  [ ] Stored in sealed, labeled container
  [ ] Kept away from moisture

Fragrance/Essential Oils:
  [ ] Scent matches expected profile
  [ ] No discoloration
  [ ] Cap seals tight

Dry Ingredients (Baking Soda, Citric Acid, Cornstarch):
  [ ] No clumping
  [ ] Free-flowing
  [ ] No off-odor

Colorants (Micas, Oxides):
  [ ] Color matches expected shade
  [ ] No clumps or hard chunks
