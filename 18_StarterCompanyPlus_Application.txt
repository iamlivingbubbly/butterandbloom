PART 1 - PASTE-READY APPLICATION NARRATIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q: Describe your business idea and what it does.
Butter & Bloom is a handcrafted bath-and-body company based in Cornwall,
Ontario, producing cold-process soap, bath bombs, shower steamers, sugar
scrub, lip balm and body butter in small batches. Production happens in an
accessible home studio designed around my needs as an entrepreneur living
with a disability. Every product is made to a written formula and batch
record, and labelled for full ingredient transparency. I sell at local
farmers' markets, to Cornwall-area customers, and online, where customers
can build their own product using a four-step designer: collection,
product, scent and size, with a fragrance pyramid (top, middle, base notes)
for each collection. One production batch makes 802 units across six
products with a retail value of $5,688.

Q: What makes you and your business ready to succeed?
I have already built the operating foundation for this business:
- Complete formulations and ingredient costings for all six products
- A production workflow, QC checklist, and batch-record system - so quality
  is consistent from bar to bar
- A live online store with product pages, fragrance storytelling and a
  custom product-builder tool
- Prepared Health Canada CNF ingredient lists for regulatory compliance
- A real costing model showing a 74% gross margin per batch
As a person living with a disability, I've designed the business to be
viable on my terms: an accessible home workspace, made-to-order batches
that match my energy and capacity, and no reliance on external labour. That
design discipline carries into the business plan itself - the numbers are
conservative, the costs are known, and the plan to sell is concrete.

Q: What will you use the grant (up to $5,000) for?
1. Bilingual labels + bulk packaging (2 batches) ......... $2,000
2. Market stall kit (table, canopy, display, card reader)  $1,200
3. Digital storefront upgrades + product photography ...... $800
4. Health Canada CNF filing + compliance materials .......  $500
5. Product liability insurance (first year) ..............  $500
TOTAL ................................................................ $5,000

Q: What is your contribution (25% match - $1,250)?
- Cash: ingredient purchase for the first market-ready batch ......... $450
- In-kind: ~40 hours of my own production setup, testing and labelling
  (valued at $800) already completed and documented
Cash + in-kind total .............. $1,250

Q: What are your goals at the end of the 12-week program?
- Complete the program training and a formal business plan + financial
  forecast (I have the numbers; the program gives them structure)
- Pitch and obtain the competitive Starter Company Plus grant
- Launch my first paid market sale (Cornwall-area market date booked)
- File my Health Canada CNF and complete bilingual labelling
- Register the business name and set up sales tax accounts (done early in
  the program)
- Bank my first three batches of retail sales (~$17,000 revenue)

Q: How will your business grow locally after the grant?
Cornwall and SDG Counties have strong farmers'-market and boutique-gifting
culture, and the Ottawa corridor adds an online-shipping market. The
grant's equipment and inventory lift lets me produce two full batches
consistently, build a photo-ready product line, and take Butter & Bloom
from home testing into weekly markets. It also supports one more goal I
believe matters: showing that an accessible, small-batch maker can run a
real, profitable local business - and I'd like to be an example of that in
Cornwall.
