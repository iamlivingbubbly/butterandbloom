Handcrafted Vegan Bath & Body Care
Cornwall, Ontario, Canada
Prepared: September 2026

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Butter & Bloom ("B&B") is a small-batch, vegan, handcrafted bath and body care
brand based in Cornwall, Ontario. We make six product categories — cold-process
soap, bath bombs, shower steamers, sugar scrub, whipped body butter, and lip
balm — all formulated in-house, cured/made by hand, and sold with full traceability
(every batch carries a code and a date).

The ask: $20,000. $7,015 to cover startup costs (equipment, initial ingredients,
packaging, licensing, marketing launch) and ~$13,000 working capital for the
first two production cycles and grant-matching. Funding strategy is stack-and-build:
Starter Company Plus grants ($5,000), Futurpreneur Start-Up Loan (up to $25,000),
and owner cash — no equity given away.

Model at a glance (one $450 ingredient batch -> 802 products):
  Revenue        $5,688
  COGS (full)    $1,457  (ingredients $450 + packaging/labour $1,007)
  Gross profit   $4,231   (74.4% gross margin)
  At current pricing; margins climb to ~84% when selling market/pickup
  (no per-unit shipping), per our cost model.

Base case (3-year): 12 -> 24 -> 36 batches/year. Revenue $68k -> $137k -> $205k,
all generated by one home-studio maker, retail+market+wholesale mix. Break-even
incl. a modest owner draw is ~0.5 batches/month (~410 units/mo); cash
break-even lower — both well within the first year (base plan runs 1.0/mo).

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PROBLEM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mainstream bath/body products are: ingredient-opaque (long laundry lists and
undisclosed fragrance), mass-produced (identikit), and full of tradeoffs (cruelty
vs. performance, "natural" claims without proof). Shoppers who want vegan,
honest, small-batch care must choose between convenience and craft — or pay
prestige prices.

B&B's wedge: a zero-compromise vegan line with PROOF of what is in every jar
(full labelling + batch codes), at a price midpoint between supermarket and
luxury, made locally in Ontario.

6. BUSINESS MODEL & PRICING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Channels: farmers'/craft markets (margins ~84% on market mix, no shipping),
Wix online store (shipping ~$3.50/unit drag -> push bundles + pickup), wholesale
boutiques (40% margin retained below retail), custom/wedding (project pricing,
70%+ margin).
Pricing doctrine (costing tool): never below max(4.5x ingredient cost, full
COGS / 0.55). "Your prices" tier [8/6/10/6/3/10] yields 74.4% gross; a
recommended tier [9/7/11/7/4/12] improves on all items.

Key unit economics (usage): ingredient ~$0.56/unit. Packaging is the biggest
COGS lever (~$1.01/unit): -$0.30/unit packaging saves ~$240/batch. Shipping kills
singles: sell 4-item bundles or pickup to protect margin.

11. RISKS & MITIGATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Market saturation of handmade soap   -> collection-level design, scent
                                         architecture, batch traceability differ.

                                         part-time helper model at 2x volume.
  Shipping erodes online margin         -> bundles, pickup, market-first mix.
  Regulation (allergen list growth)     -> allergen-aware formulas (file 13),
                                         budgeted CNF re-submissions.
  Grant/loan timing                     -> SCP Spring 2027 + Futurpreneur
                                         application now; owner cash runway.
  Health: founder has a disability      -> ODSP self-employment rules consulted;
                                         DTC/RDSP + Canada Disability Benefit
