BUTTER & BLOOM — BRAND COLLECTIONS REFERENCE
===============================================================================

Standard Operating Procedure: BRAND-COL-001
Effective Date: ____________  Version: 1.0

The store catalog organizes every product into one or more of three branded
collections. Each collection has its own aesthetic tone, which should guide
label design, scent selection, packaging, and product photography.

===============================================================================
COLLECTION 1: SOFT GLOW
===============================================================================

Keywords: Gentle · Soft · Natural
Feels like: Calming, nurturing, skin-first, botanical

Design Direction:
| Element | Guidance |
|---------|----------|
| Color palette | Warm neutrals, cream, soft blush, sage, muted rose |
| Typography | Organic, rounded serif or handwritten script |
| Label stock | Natural kraft, matte finish |
| Imagery | Botanicals, flowers, soft-focus textures |
| Accents | Gold or warm bronze detail |

Scent Direction: Lavender, chamomile, rose, oatmilk & honey, vanilla, calendula

Suggested Products for this Collection:
- Lavender Oat Milk Soap
- Rose Clay Soap
- Vanilla Bean Sugar Scrub
- Lavender Dream Body Butter
- Rose Garden Bath Bomb
- Lavender Shower Steamer

===============================================================================
COLLECTION 2: SWEET PLAYFUL
===============================================================================

Keywords: Cute · Cheerful · Whimsical
Feels like: Fun, bright, giftable, joyful

Design Direction:
| Element | Guidance |
|----------|----------|
| Color palette | Pastels, candy pinks, mint, lemon, soft coral |
| Typography | Playful, rounded, hand-drawn lettering |
| Label stock | White or light glossy/matte |
| Imagery | Sprinkles, sparkles, doodle-style illustrations |
| Accents | Glitter-free but playful foil or colored elements |

Scent Direction: Citrus, berry, bubblegum-style fragrance oils, candy, fruity blends

Suggested Products for this Collection:
- Citrus Sunrise Bath Bomb
- Citrus Burst Shower Steamer
- Citrus Sugar Scrub
- Berry Lip Balm
- Sweet scented fragrance-oil soaps

===============================================================================
COLLECTION 3: RESET MINIMAL
===============================================================================

Keywords: Clean · Restrained · Modern
Feels like: Simple, uncluttered, premium, design-forward

Design Direction:
| Element | Guidance |
|----------|----------|
| Color palette | White, black, grey, single accent tone |
| Typography | Sans-serif, clean, generous whitespace |
| Label stock | Matte white or uncoated |
| Imagery | Minimal, geometric, empty space |
| Accents | None or single-color details only |

Scent Direction: Eucalyptus, mint, tea tree, unscented, fresh green, coastal

Suggested Products for this Collection:
- Eucalyptus Mint Soap
- Tea Tree Charcoal Soap
- Eucalyptus Mint Shower Steamer
- Mint Lip Balm
- Sinus Relief Shower Steamer
