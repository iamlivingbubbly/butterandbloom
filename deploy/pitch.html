Proof, not promises

Every jar can be verified.

Batch-traceable

Every product carries a batch code + date. Write it in, we tell you exactly what went into it — and when.

Ingredient-exact

Full INCI lists, bilingual labelling (Health Canada), fragrance allergens disclosed — now 24, moving to 81.

Formulation discipline

Written formulas, batch records, QC pass before anything sells. Made to a standard, not a whim.

Vegan & cruelty-free

Every ingredient plant-based and printed in full. No asterisks.

Business model

Diversified channels, margin-first.

Markets / pickup

~84% gross, no shipping. Local-first loyalty engine.

Online bundles

~70–72% net. 4-item bundles beat singles on shipping.

Wholesale boutiques

~65% margin below retail. Shelf reach, repeat orders.

Custom / wedding

~70%+ project margin. High ticket, low effort.

Single-item online sales are deliberately the price-bait, not the profit engine.
